# Paywalled canon & books — external links

Original texts we could not redistribute (publisher-paywalled or books).
All are synthesized in `WHAT_WE_LEARNT.md`; search Google Scholar with the
titles below for institutional access.

## Foundational stochastic-games theory

- **Shapley, "Stochastic Games", PNAS 1953** — the founding paper.
  https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.39.10.1095 (open on PNAS in some
  regions; PDF blocked our fetch)
- **Mertens & Neyman, "Stochastic games", IJGT 1981** — value exists without
  discounting. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01768755
- **Solan & Vieille, "Stochastic games", PNAS 2015** (survey).
  https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1514951112
- **Hart & Mas-Colell, "A simple adaptive procedure leading to correlated
  equilibrium", Econometrica 2000** — regret matching.
  https://doi.org/10.2307/2999445
- **Blackwell, "An analog of the minimax theorem for vector payoffs",
  Ann. Math. Stat. 1956** — approachability.
  https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177728342
- **Foster & Vohra, "Calibrated learning and correlated equilibrium",
  GEB 1997.** https://doi.org/10.1006/game.1997.0585

## Russian school (books / journals)

- **Pontryagin, Boltyanskii, Gamkrelidze, Mishchenko, *The Mathematical
  Theory of Optimal Processes*, 1961** — the maximum principle.
- **Krasovskii & Subbotin, *Game-Theoretical Control Problems*, Springer
  1988 (Russian original 1974)** — positional differential games, stable
  bridges.
- **Tsetlin, *Automaton Theory and Modeling of Biological Systems*, 1973**;
  Krinsky & Tsetlin 1963 automata-games work.
- **Vapnik, *The Nature of Statistical Learning Theory*, 1995 /
  *Statistical Learning Theory*, 1998** — VC dimension, SRM.
- **Vovk, "Aggregating strategies", COLT 1990**; **Shafer & Vovk, *Probability
  and Finance: It's Only a Game!*, 2001** — game-theoretic probability.
- **Petrosyan & Zaccour, "Time-consistent Shapley value allocation of
  pollution cost reduction", JEDC 2003** — time-consistency.
- **Ermoliev, *Stochastic Programming / Stochastic Quasigradient Methods*,
  IIASA 1983.**
- **Markov 1906 (chains); Kolmogorov 1933 (probability axioms)** — the
  substrate.
